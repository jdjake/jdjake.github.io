Session 2

23rd September, 2026

Players

  • Ben (Playing Lord Mogglin)
  • Jaco (Playing Nokko)
  • Joe (Playing Hilda)
  • Kai (Playing Lan)
  • Mungo (Playing Sprigrot)
  • Ross (Playing ?)

XP Earned:

458 XP (Exploration) + 38 XP (Encounters) + 516 XP (Treasure) = 1012 XP Each

Summary

Frisk, The 5th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Cloudy and Misty

  • The party continued their search through Droomen Knoll for Arda Vague and the source of the eerie pipe music that lured him to the old Fairy Fort.
    • In the Fort’s Courtyard, Lord Mogglin and Hilda enchanted two drunken Crookhorns and convinced them their companions had robbed them. A brawl erupted, giving the party an opening to recover their belongings from under the Fort’s gatehouse. Nokko and Hilda subdued the remaining Crookhorns with Mogglin’s twine, but Hilda took a vicious goring, that would soon infect her with The Dreaded Goat Froth.
    • Lord Mogglin charmed Grerta, a Crookhorn stationed in the lookout tower, into helping them search the keep’s dungeons. Grerta led Mogglin, Sprigrot, and ? snuck past a ragged, two-headed, purple-wattled cockerel guarding the main entrance to the den of Grobnott, the head of the Crookhorn gang stationed at the Fort, heading for the side-entrance to Grobnott’s den around the corner.
    • The party discovered a frosted-sundial, several cells, and the source of the eerie music: Famine-Whistles-Whither, a thin, ragged Frost-Elf with tangled thistledown-like hair and twisted, claw-like fingernails. He played panpipes carved from the bones of rats he’d caught in his cell with the hope of finding some mortals to free him from his cell.
      • A powerful magical seal barred his cell, blue flames shimmering whenever anyone touched it. Famine-Whistles-Whither promised treasure and friendship if the party freed him and helped him ‘Find Mallowheart’s Mercy’.
      • The party learned that Prince Mallowheart, half-brother of the Cold Prince, had once ruled Droomen Knoll. He’d take courtiers here to feast and make merry. Frost-covered murals in another room depicted his courtiers:
        • Prince Seven-Past-Noon: A tall, bitter nobleman, face blackened with frostbite and wearing a crown of thorns and diamonds.
        • Lady Silver-So-Mirrored slender, playing a harp shaped like a teardrop-headed angel.
        • Sir Dawn-Never-Blooms, a tall knight in frost-clad armor and a beard of icicles.
        • Lord Slumber-Bone, a portly man with wide eyes and a merry expression.
        • Whisper-of-Candlelight, a statuesque woman with longsword and shield.
        • Famine-Whistles-Whither, a slender man with thistledown hair, playing a silver flute. A trio of blood-red hounds snapped at his heels.
      • Famine-Whistles-Whither revealed he was once Mallowheart’s fool. He fell from the Prince’s favor after baking the Prince’s hunting hounds into a pie and feeding them to him as a joke. The prince locked him away here to be forgotten.
      • Not wanting to deal with his shenanigans, the party decided not to free him. Famine-Whistles-Whither cursed them, promising vengeance once he was freed.
    • Grerta slipped into the dungeon chamber where Grobnott held Arda. She retrieved Arda without alerting him. Grobnott lay deep in a drugged trance, inhaling rotting smoke from yellow, greasy candles that surrounded him. Arda seemed heavily drugged himself.
      • Grobnott eventually awoke and pursued the party into the courtyard. Lan hurled the dungeon cockerel at him, prompting the creature to release a horrible fart in fear that left Grobnott keeled over, vomiting. The party bound him, while Nokko climbed the tower and pushed a cracked Merlon on top of him, crushing him and the cockerel.
      • In Grobnott’s den, the party found a scrawled note in Gaffe instructing someone to contact Rodger Burl in Dreg regarding spies within the constabulary.
    • Another chamber in the dungeon contained a nest of Striges and a corpse drained by dozens of puncture wounds. The corpse carried a golden owl torc, a Drunic symbol, and an orange crystal. Picking up the crystal, Nokko had a vision of a slime-coated stag skeleton twitching within a circle of trilithons, accompanied by a grim voice: “Ill omens at the Whything Stones. Winter cometh.”
  • The party began their journey back to Dreg. Southeast of Droomen Knoll, the party discovered a rickety willow stilt-house in the marshes belonging to Tekwell, an elderly Longhorn with dirty fur, sickly yellow eyes, and a single horn protruding from beneath a wide-brimmed straw hat, the other broken off.
    • Tekwell fishes up strange debris washed up from the boats and barges that travel down the Hameth, trading between Dreg, the Woodcutter’s Encampment, and the southern lands outside of the Dolmenwood, brought up by the ship-conveyor at the Falls of Nyf. He only had fish to sell at the moment, but he welcomed the party to return later, when he might have found a more interesting catch.
    • He warned the party about the Hag, who wanders the swamps: “Fools try and reason with the Hag, but the price she demands in exchange for her help is too big.”
  • Hilda and Lan have caught The Dreaded Goat Froth, Lan from sleeping in the Crookhorn’s flea infested bedrolls. They’ll lose a point of Wisdom or Intelligence each day until either is reduced to 2, at which point they enter an uncontrollably violent frenzy.
  • In the morning, Lord Mogglin told Grerta to run at full speed into the swamp, hoping she’d be completely lost by the time she broke free of Mogglin’s enchantment.
