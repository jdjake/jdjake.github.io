Session 6

21st October, 2026

Players

  • Carina (Playing Schmold Fernhead)
  • Joe (Playing Hank Do-Good)
  • Jaco (Playing Nokko)
  • Kai (Playing Ser Garfunkel)
  • Mungo (Playing Sprigrot)
  • Gary (Playing Supper-Before-Noon)

XP Earned:

505 XP (Exploration)
59 XP (Encounters)

564 XP Each

Summary

Sunning the 14th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Drizzly and Damp

  • Supper-Before-Noon, Schmold, and Hank met the party travelling through Hag’s Addle from Dreg. They told the party about the Bonfires of the Vanities happening in that city.
  • Investigated the glowing green cave in Hex 1108. We called out to announce our arrival to anyone in the cave. A grumpy voice responded. She didn’t want to be disturbed, and threatened to curse us if we came near.
    • We went in anyway. The cave had several chambers, the ground squelching mud, with dim, green candle-light positioned around. In the second chamber, a witch brewed a cauldron over a fire, amidst various supplies: drying herbs and pickle jars. Her name was Bragwen Hoad.
    • She threatened us, but when she heard we needed help with Ser Garfunkel’s curse (and especially when she heard about our suspicions of the Mother Baker outside Prigwort), she warmed up to us considerably.
    • The Mother Baker (her daughters in particular) had stolen her wand, and if we retrieved it, she’d cure Ser Garfunkel of his curse. We made deal to return her wand, in exchange for clearing a curse, and potentially to make an ally that can help with Nokko’s sentient pig.
  • We began travelling back to the Mother Baker to retrieve Bragwen’s wand. At night, while the rest prepared camp, Supper-Before-Noon hunted the delicious sugary flesh-slime of the Dolmenwood Honey Badger. But their arrow missed, and the badgers escaped.

Colly the 15th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Frosty and Chill

  • We returned to the Baker’s House outside Prigwort. Nokko and Hank knocked on the front door, hoping to distract Mother while Ser Garfunkel, disguised as a cat, snuck round the back of the house. Mother was not pleased to see Nokko again. And when Schmold and Supper-Before-Noon knocked again to further distract, Mother warned them to leave immediately to avoid trouble.
    • In the back belched a 60’ pool of yeasty, frothing dough. Six gingerbread men with vacant icing grins on their faces mindlessly stirred the pool, ocasionally wheelbarrowing dough to deliver into the kitchen. They payed no attention to Garfunkel, and when they delivered dough into the kitchen, he snuck past.
    • In the kitchen, the daughters worked dough, delivering baking to an enormous brick oven. Shelves brimmed with baking, spices, and flour. Garfunkel attempted to sneak past, but the daughters saw the cat, and crowded around to have a stroke.
    • The two bards Nokko and Hank took position at each sides of the front entrance and began playing a tune. Mother told the daughters to head to their rooms for safety, opened the door, and swung her rolling pin, ordering the Gingerbread Men from the back to attack. Four more half-formed men emerged from the pool to attack the two bards.
    • Meanwhile, Ser Garfunkel slunk up to the front door, and talked to several cats that lounged in the rafters of the foyer. They weren’t willing to help Garfunkel take down their owner - they had a comfortable life here.
    • Supper-Before-Noon shot Mother, causing her to close the door and retreat, directly into the face of Ser Garfunkel, transforming back into humanoid form.
    • Before Ser Garfunkel could attack, Mother cast a spell, encasing Ser Garfunkel in magical pastry. Mother began dragging him towards the oven.
    • The others attempted to fight off the Gingerbread Men, but they proved too tough. Several party members were severely injured. Leaving Debbie for dead, the group retreated to the pool, hoping to distract the Gingerbread Men while Sprigrot dealt with Mother.
