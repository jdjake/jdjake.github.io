Session 2

30th September, 2026

Players

  • Ben (Playing Lord Mogglin)
  • Jaco (Playing Nokko)
  • Kai (Playing Lan and Sir Garfunkel)
  • Mungo (Playing Sprigrot)

XP Earned:

660 XP (Exploration) + 70 XP (Encounters) + 100 XP (Treasure) = 830 XP Each

Summary

Eggfast, The 6th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Clement and Balmy

  • The party travelled along the Hameth riverbank from Tekwell’s Stilt House back to Dreg.
    • Along the way, Arda revealed that he had visited the Fort several times before the Crookhorns captured him, suggesting they had only recently taken up residence there.
    • On the journey back to Dreg, Lan, who grew up in Dreg, told the party about how Dreg had always been a pretty seedy place, but recently has become more violent and dangerous. Brother Rodger Hogbeard, a friar, has taken to patrolling the town with religious vigilantes to dispense retributive justice on sinners.
    • Arrived back in Dreg at night, returning Arda to his parents, who offered a reward for the group’s help.
    • That evening, Lord Mogglin, Nokko, and Lan crossed the river by candlelit boat to Chateau Shantywood, a grand manor on an island in the middle of the Hameth.
      • Mogglin attempted to persuade Madame Shantywood, the Chateau’s frank and self-assured proprietor, to let him perform a play. When she refused, he swaggered over to the Chateau’s Ballroom, charmed a guard into securing the stage from the performing child minstrel, dimming the lights. He performed an elaborate love story between a pair of Frost-Elf and Human men, with gratuitous sexual scenes. Upon finishing, he turned invisible, effectively vanishing from the scene. The Minstrel resumed his performance, but Mogglin had taken most of the energy from the room with his disappearance.
      • Madame Shantywood confronted Mogglin in his chambers afterwards. She was unmoved by the spectacle, not directly mad at Mogglin, but firing the guard he charmed. Mogglin tried to hire her as a retainer the following morning as she boarded a boat with her belongings back to Dreg, but she had realized he had charmed her and flatly refused.
    • Sprigrot spent the evening sampling Dreg’s famous bog-swine sausages while inquiring about Brother Hogbeard. A vendor revealed that the Reverend Mother wanted the friar driven from town. Sprigrot then retired to the communal beds at The Spawning Salmon.

Sunning, the 7th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Clear and Chilly

  • Met back up in town, and headed to Church of St. Wick to see if the Reverend Mother could help with Lan and Hilda’s affliction.
    • The Church was built on a pier on the Hameth, mouldering, battered, and poorly maintained. The Reverend Mother seemed more interested in her books than helping out the public. She just wants to be left in peace and for Dreg to return to the quiet seediness it had been before Brother Hogbeard arrived in town. It doesn’t seem like St. Wick’s miracles could help.
    • St. Wick the Venerable worked as a bargeman, ferrying passengers across a great lake and entertaining them with religious parables along the way. When a belligerent knight became angered and impaled Wick upon his lance, the saint-to-be removed himself from the weapon’s shaft and continued preaching, the wound miraculously healed. His miracles can heal great wounds, but not treat disease.
    • The Mother told the party that St. Pastery’s that they may have better luck in Prigwort. St. Pastery was a vicar turned mendicant. Refused entry to the castle of an ailing Prince Wallobringe during an outbreak of plague, St. Pastery blessed the hands of all butchers and cow-herders within a ten mile radius, ensuring that all meat brought to the ailing prince restoring him to health despite his refusal to admit Pastery.
    • The Mother told the party that Brother Hogbeard arrived in town five years ago, looking to help the poor and needy. But he’s recently succumbed to madness, turning to firebrand justice, cleansing the sinner with the zealots he’s attracted from the townsfolk.
  • Travelled to the Fish Market, where Brother Hogbeard and his followers patrolled, preaching. The Fishmongers mostly continued hawking their wares, hoping not to be confronted by Hogbeard.
    • Lan pleaded his case to Hogbeard. Hogbeard, with crazed, unblinking eyes, told Lan that sin can only be cleansed with fire. Lan, perhaps slightly addled by the Goat Froth, convinced him to setting a great fire. The Zealots took wood from an unlucky Fishmonger’s stall, using it to build a great bonfire. Lan was tied to the top, and burned alive. Hogbeard declared that, if Lan was a true believer, he would not have burned, taking this as proof of Lan’s sins.
  • Found Lord Javier Thistlewhistle, a member of the Brackenwold court, arriving at the Port of Dreg from Chateau Shantywood, and preparing to make leave on his pleasure barge to the Woodcutter’s Encampment, where he would take a carriage along the Camp Road to Castle Brackenwold. Thoroughly impressed by Mogglin’s performance, Thistlewhistle invited the party to join him on the barge Mist, and someday entertain him at Court.
    • After disembarking at the Camp, Thistlewhistle ordered the boat’s driver, Otis, to take the party north to Fog’s lake, where the party hoped to disembark and head to Prigwort. Otis wasn’t very comfortable about heading to Fog Lake, it being rumored to be haunting by bog-owls and drunic magic, but followed Thistlewhistle’s ordered.
    • Along the river, the party came across a small tributary leading to a network of grottoes 120’ away from the Hameth. Perched atop a rock above this grotto, a man in staff and robes conversed with a Yickerwill, a 6’ flying creature, with legs like caterpillars and faces like moths, and icy, fluttering wings. Seeing the boat pass, the Yickerwill flew at great speed. Fortunately, Mogglin charmed the Yickerwill, preventing it from attacking the party. The robed man seemed annoyed at his conversation being interrupted, but slunk away into the woods.
    • The Party arrived at Fog Lake, and waded onto the misty shores. On the shore, a man in courtly breeches and powdered wig sat crosslegged, cooking down a pan of noxious crystal sludge. He quickly fled upon seeing the Yickerwill, allowing the party to steal his belongings. Sprigrot communed with the roots of a nearby willow, who revealed that the man was cooking up drugs harvested from crystals in a nearby cave.
  • The Party headed north, finding the road West to Prigwort, along with a thatched cottage in a glade just a mile off the road. Too tired to travel further that day, they knocked on the cottage door, hoping for shelter. A small child greeted them, calling for her mother. ‘Mother’ greeted the party, a plump, middle-aged woman. She was pleasant to the party, and welcomed them to sleep in the glade, and to partake in her baking. But she could not allow them to sleep in her home, protective of her twelve daughters, and of their baking recipes.
    • The party taunted her with the charmed Yickerwill, forcing her to kill it. She cast a magic spell that turned the Yickerwill to gingerbread, which she quickly smashed with her rolling pin after the party refused to help in killing the creature. Annoyed at the party’s lack of help, Mother told the group to clear off.
  • The party were forced to camp out in the woods away from the glade, but didn’t manage to secure enough firewood to last them through the night. In the witching hour, while Sir Garfunkel was on watch, something glimmered in the distance; a campfire across a grove of trees, the piercing glow of wild animal eyes, and two men in glittering, frost-rimed armor…
