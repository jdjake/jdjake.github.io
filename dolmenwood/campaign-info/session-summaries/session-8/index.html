Session 8

4th November, 2026

Players

  • Ben (Playing Lord Mogglin)
  • Carina (Playing Schmold Fernhead)
  • Jaco (Playing Nokko)
  • Kai (Playing Ser Garfunkel)

1766 XP (Exploration)
60 XP (Encounters)
0 XP (Treasure)

1826 XP Each

Summary

Moot the 25th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Frosty and Chill

  • In the evening, Austache and several bounders escorted two strange knights in gold plate armour atop blonde wolves to the Wrinkled Medlar, where Lord Mogglin and Nokko were performing. Austache interrupted the performance and demanded the party surrender for imprisonment over the theft of the Baker’s equipment.
    • Mogglin polymorphed Austache into a rat, which Garfunkel caught before it fled. The audience panicked and began evacuating the Medlar.
    • Garfunkel went out to the knights, telling that, on his honor, he’d go find the others in the pub. Meanwhile Nokko and Mogglin escaped through the backdoor, untied their swine, and snuck off at a brisk pace, heading for the Turret Gate on the southeast side of the city.
    • Garfunkel reemerged, trying to get to his horse, tied to the front of the building. The two knights began to suspect him, so he ran upstairs with his retainer Arfred. He bolted his door shut and tried to put on as much of his armor on before the two knights burst through. He had his breastplate and chainmail tunic on when the two knights burst through.
    • Garfunkel and Arfred managed to kill the two knights, to an audience of jeering peasants from the window below. Garfunkel bit the head off of Austache (as rat), killing him. Yet as Garfunkel and Arfred prepared to leave, they were caught by six more guards when they fled out the backdoor. Garfunkel fled in chester form, leaving Arfred to fend for himself. We’re not sure what happened to him.
    • Four guards stood at the Turret Gate to Prigwort. Mogglin swayed one, and Nokko lead the other three into the forest with the music.
      • In a golden-beeched forest to the north of Prigwort, Nokko stopped playing his tune, leaving one of the four other guards charmed. To scare the other two off, Mogglin turned a guard’s sword into a snake, causing him to drop it in fear.
  • Headed north to get away from town, and camped in the woods.

Chime the 26th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Frosty and Chill

  • In the morning, discovered Harrowmoor Keep, over craggy gorges along the Groaning Loch.
  • Could not take Garfunkel’s Hop Clopper over the gorges, so were forced to double back to find the road to Harrowmoor.
  • On the road, Mogglin was distracted by the moaning of dead men and infants, then later on the road, by a groggy confused feeling, as if being awoken from a dream. Lady Harrowmoor later informed him that this is likely the effect of crossing over a ley line in the Dolmenwood. It may be useful to chart out these ley lines, as certain magic is enhanced when casting spells near leylines, and at the intersections of ley lines stand great nodal stones where one can harness and learn great arcane magicks.
  • Far off the road, the party saw a humid, warm, misty glade containing a huge, shallow pool of placid, lilypadded water. A tall, black obelisk stood from the centre of the glade. Lady Harrowmoor told the party this was the Gorthstone.
    • Garfunkel was the only brave enough to investigate. In the pool, a pale, shrouded woman drifted amongst the reeds. Garfunkel threw a stone at the pool, and was struck with an intense urge to drift to sleep. The woman emerged from the pool, a spectral woman with eerie bright blue eyes and dressed in sodden white vestments. She pleaded with Garfunkel to help her. Garfunkel ran off, ignoring her pleas, and the lady’s screams turned angry. But Garfunkel got away without any harm.
  • Made it to Harrowmoor Keep. Mogglin dropped his Thesbian credits, which managed to secure the party with quarters for the night.
    • Lady Harrowmoor liked some of Mogglin’s more historical and dry poetry, especially his play about Grupert IV, the uncle of the current Duke of Brackenwold, Thespian III (a bit like Shakespeare’s Richard II).
    • While Mogglin performed some of his work, Nokko and Schmold snuck around Harrowmoor Keep, looking for curious things to investigate. They managed to find her diary in her quarters. But at the sound of footsteps, Nokko and Schmold hid under the bed and wardrobe, but were found by the guards, looking for Mildred and Puggsley, Harrowmoor’s two and four year old children. They talked their way out of suspicion, claiming to be playing the game with the children.
    • The most recent entries in the diary primarily documented Lady Harrowmoor’s investigation into the disappearance of her six year old daughter Violet. Alongside conventional enquiries, such as sending guards to see if any of the nearby inns reported seeing her, she recorded the following entry from the last new moon: ‘Dreamed with the lake Forroth last night. I will jump at shadows for several days now, and will not sleep for the nightmares. But it knew where Violet had gone. She has been taken to the Abbey of St. Clewyd.’
    • Harrowmoor apologized for the lack of food, serving the party only bread and water late at night. Tonight is the ‘feast’ of St. Howarth the Accursed. Originally St. Howarth the Just, Howarth betrayed the archangel Heroclesius to the clutches of devils. Howarth was cast out from heaven and all of his miracles in the mortal world were reversed. This is a day of mourning, fasting, and penitence. Most religious folk stay at home.
