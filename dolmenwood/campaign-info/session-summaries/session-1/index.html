Session 1

16th September, 2026

Players

  • Ben (Playing Lord Mogglin)
  • Jaco (Playing Nokko)
  • Joe (Playing Hank Do-Good)
  • Kai (Playing Lan)
  • Kasturi (Playing Hilda)
  • Vlad (Playing Schmold)

XP Earned:

220 XP (Exploration)
6 XP (Encounters)
0 XP (Treasure)

226 XP Each

Summary

Moot, The 4th of Reedwyrme (Feast of St. Elephantine the scribe)

Weather is Balmy and Clement

  • Met in the wide halls of The Spawning Salmon, a squat, converted warehouse in the rough port-town of Dreg, along the wide, shallow river Hameth. It’s the prime gambling establishment in Dreg. The bard’s performed, while the other members of the group gambled.
    • Gamblers talked about the Vague family’s problems, a human family from Dreg. One of their sons, Arda, has gone missing. He’d spent several nights last week visiting Droomen Knoll, an old ruined Fairy fort to the north, on the border’s of Hag’s Addle along the Hameth, where he’d heard enchanting music. The Vagues worry Arda has been kidnapped by fairies. They’ll give two hundred gold for Arda’s safe return.
    • Word among the gamblers is that Crookhorns have been snooping around to the north of late, corrupted Breggle that serve the Nag Lord.
  • Travelled north along the shores of the Hameth.
    • Surprised by a boar, clearing out nearby swampland to make a den in preparation for winter. The boar became territorial, and the party attacked before it could charge.
    • The boar was slain, its tusks taken for trophies, and bacon for dinner. But not before the boar charged into Schmold, cracking a rib (it will take eight days for this to heal).
    • Cleared out the boar’s den to make shelter for the night. Yet when the mists came in across the Hameth the night got pretty chilly without bedrolls.

Frisk, The 5th of Reedwyrme

Weather is Cloudy and Misty

  • Arrived at Droomen Knoll, confirming the rumours of snooping Crookhorns. Four were camped inside the ruined fort, drunk and playing raucous music, accompanying an eerie piping from below. Another watched from the fort’s sole remaining tower.
    • The Crookhorns were wary of us, so we left our belongings by the entrance archway, keeping only the bards’ instruments, to show that we meant them no harm.
    • One of the drunken Crookhorn’s let slip that they’d seen a boy around the Fort, though the other was quick to shut them up when they revealed this information. Suspicious…
    • Two of the four Crookhorns were enchanted with bardic song.
  • While the party kept the Crookhorns occupied, Schmold slipped around the side of the fort. He examined the tracks leading from the entrance, hoping to find a boy’s footprints, but the mud had been heavily trampled and he could distinguish nothing.
    • Failing this, he crept around the back of the fort and peered inside through the rubble of one of fort’s collapsed towers, observing the party charming the Crookhorns.
