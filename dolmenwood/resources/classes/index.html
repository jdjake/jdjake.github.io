Bards

Musicians and poets drawn to a life of wandering and adventure. Their Prime Abilities are Charisma and Dexterity. Their Hit Die is a d6.

As they level, they become better at Listening. They also have three additional Bardic skills: Decipher Documents allows them to get the gist of non-magical texts in a language they do not speak, Legerdemain allows them to perform sleight of hand tricks, and Monster Lore allows them to identify monsters and their basic powers and vulnerabilities based on their appearances in Myth and Folklore.

Once per turn, Bards can play music to Counter Charm, giving allies immunity to magical effects based on music and song, and +2 to Saving Throws against Fairy Magic. Once per day per level, Bards may Enchant creatures to Fascinate them, causing them to devote utter attention to the bards song, and follow them while the Bard plays their song.

Cleric

Holy warriors in the service of the Pluritine Church. Their Prime Ability is Wisdom. Their Hit Die is a d6. They must be Lawful or Neutral in Alignment, and follow the Tenets of their Holy Order or fall from grace.

They may Detect Holy Magic Items by touch and concentration, cast Holy Magic at higher levels channeled through a Holy Symbol, and Turn Undead once per Turn, attempting to drive off the Undead through the might of the One True God.

Enchanter

Wanderers who wield Fairy Magic. Their Prime Abilities are Charisma and Intelligence. Their Hit Die is a d6.

Enchanters have an additional Skill, Detect Magic, which enables them to detect subtle resonances woven into an enchanted object via touch. They are granted the use of Fairy Runes, the secret sigils of the Fairy Rulers, as well as a minor magical talents known as a Glamours. However, by allying themselves with the godless world of Fairy, they are Resistant to Divine Aid.

Fighter

Mercenaries, Soldiers, and Ruffians, experienced in combat and warfare. Their Prime Abilities are Strength. Their Hit Die is a d8.

They possess Combat Talents which grant them exceptional abilities in melee and ranged combat.

Friar

Wandering Ascetics spreading the Gospel of the Pluritine Church. They are only loosely affiliated with The Church and exist outside the strict religious heirarchy of the Clergy, thus beloved by the common folk, whom they often aid when The Church does not. Their Prime Abilities are Intelligence and Wisdom. They have a Hit Die of d4. They must be Lawful or Neutral in Alignment.

A Divine Blessing gives them an Armour of Faith, giving them a bonus to AC. They have a Skill target of 5 for Survival when Foraging. They are efficient in the use of Medicinal Herbs, can cast holy Magic, and can Turn Undead once per Turn. However, they have taken a monastic vow of Poverty, and must donae excess wealth to worthy causes (not other PCs), and dress in a simple habit and cut their hair in tonsure.

Hunter

Expert trackers, stalkers, and killers, at home in the wild woods. Their Prime Ability is Constitution and Dexterity, and they have a Hit Die of d8.

A Hunter may forge a bond with an Animal Companion. Hunters have three additional skills: Alertness, which prevents them from being Surprised, Stalking, which allows them to remain undetected in the light brush of the wilds, and Tracking, which allows a Hunter to find tracks and follow them. They gain a +1 Attack Bonus to Missile Attacks, and may take Trophies from creatures they have hunted and use them as lucky charms. They are Wayfinders that are less likely to find their way when lost.

Knight

Warriors who serve a noble, doing their bidding and upholding their honor. They serve a Liege, one of the eight Lower Houses of The Dolmenwood. Their Prime Abilities are Charisma and Strength, and have a Hit Die of d8.

They are expert Horseman, easily assessing steeds and gaining a +1 Bonus to Attack while Mounted. They are Monster Slayers, gaining a +2 Bonus to Attack against Large creatures. They have Strength of Will, gaining a +2 Bonus to Saving Throws against fear-based effects.

Magician

Connoiseurs of secret arcane lore. They hone innate sparks of magical sensitivity through years of arduous study, imprinting patterns of magical energy into their mind (a process known as memorizing a spell) to be unleashed at a later date. Their Prime Ability is Intelligence, and they have a Hit Die of d4.

A Magician starts with a single Spellbook which gives them three Arcane Spells, but may only cast one a day. They have an additional skill, Detect Magic, which enables them to detect subtle resonances woven into an enchanted object, place, or creature, through touch.

Thief

Rogues who live by skills of deception and stealth. Their Prime Ability is Dexterity and their Hit Die is a d4.

Thieves are proficient in Backstabbing, dealing deadly blows when attacking from behind with a dagger. They have five additional Skills: Climb Wall allows them to climb vertical sheer surfaces with only minimal handholds without specialized equipment, Decipher Document allows them to get the gist of non-magical texts in a language they do not speak, Disarm Mechanism allows them to disarm complex, clockwork-like traps and mechanisms using Thieves Tools, Legerdemain allows them to perform sleight of hand tricks, Pick Lock allows them to open a lock without a key using Thieves Tools, and Stealth allows them to make a Stealth Check to remain undetected when shadows are the only cover.
