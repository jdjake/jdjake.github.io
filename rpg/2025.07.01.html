Resolution

Date: July 1st, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • The party goes to the top of one of the skyscrappers, through a big elevator, and we encounter a lost group that talks through bubbles, they are peaceful, but scared of us
  • They talk about the Prophet Ahkesh, and his apostles that got lost. This Prophet Ahkesh also tells poems, and tried to tell a poem from seeing the 4 Djinns.
  • Ahkesh recognizes the temple that the party arrives at. It reminds him of Gnoll architecture. It is an ancient circular city or an ancient architectual style.
  • There is a tower in the center. Something is wrong with it. The top of the tower is a cone instead of a cusp. No light escapes from the area aroud it. It has heart hex eyes, like bug eyes around it and it has vines of tongues climbing it.
  • The Gnoll, Dris, takes the party to brew some coffee. The Gnolls don't know about the heart. They got teleported into it along with the temple plaza.
  • Ru'in becomes convinced that he had an entire conversation with Magimel that never took place.
  • The party ventures into the temple. Ahkesh disarms an electric trap, dealing 16 stress to the delve.
  • Asseyn fails fighting a stone golem and takes echo fallout.
  • Ru'in deals 1 stress to the delve by .
  • Aodh deals 1 stress to the delve by sneaking by automated turrets.
  • Ru'in then bombs it with two pipe bombs, and hears a huge echo of resonance
  • The party sees the Great Resonance Chamber. It is made of metal parts of the Vermissian network, with concentric odd sided 3d meshes inscribed into spheres, and the floor is made out of tongue. There is a heart in the center floating and glowing golden light.
  • Aodh tripped with a taste bud, and then the irregular plates open like eyelids with hexagonal eyes.
    • Ahkesh is called by the heart to scream with all his power, and he does, creating a huge resonance affecting everyone. Ahkesh vibrates so much that he almost looks like 4 copies of himself.
  • Ru'in tries to knock him but he can't, and Ahkesh finally sees THE WORDS. As he tries to say them, he explodes into lighting, leaving just a chunk of metal armor behind, along a perpetual motion machine that no longer moves.
  • The lighting goes to the eyes, and 7 creatures get out of the eyes, screaming in horrible notes.
  • The group is able to escape, but at a cost. When they arrive Dris, the lost follower of the Prophet Ahkesh, explains that everything happened as the prophecy foretold... the false prophet saw THE WORDS, but he couldn't say THE POEM, as The Heart takes in the vision before Ahkesh could do anything. But now, in the last of days, Ahkesh will be heard to the lost, guiding them with his poems.
  • Magimel appears, and Ru'in confronts her, realizing that she had nothing to do with the bloodrot.
  • Aodh goes to hug her sneakily, but he actually burns her with the Fire of the Red King. She tries to attack him back with her control, but Aodh doesn't fall to the mind control and continues to burn her.
  • Ru'in and Asseyn seeing this decide to bail, this is way over their heads. And they hear the voice of Ahkesh guiding them out with a poem.
  • There are spiders everywhere moving Aodh, he burns them all, but it is not enough. But then, Aodh accepts his inner greed as his defining trait, and finally becomes the Red King, a mighty huge Red Dragon, with scaly exterior and all-powerful fire that can destroy everything. The Red King engulfs everything in flames, and consumes everything. Even the woman that he first encounter in the Heart. Even the Dollmakers, and even Vector.
  • He burns even the chamber he is in, and needs to go back to the dimension where the Red King first came, so he steps out of our dimension into the dimension of fire, where only the Red King exists, becoming the fire itself, always burning in this temple, creating new glass sculptures of Ahkesh, Ru'in, Asseyn and a tiny sculpture of Vector. Also an Aodh is there.
  • Asseyn leaves following Ahkesh's voice. He is never quite the same, and lives a dull life full of fear.
  • Ru'in stays always searching for the bloodrot that doesn't exist.
  • Ahkesh leads the people that are lost out of the Heart.
