The Burning of the Library

Date: June 2nd, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • Asseyn receives rings after rummaging through the piglet nest.
  • There is a hut made of clay behind the pigs. There are two people in the hut that the party detects. They are drow women. They have mischiefous smiles and they have skull belts that look like human bones.
  • Aodh uses Greed of the Red King to detect their desires in this moment. They want to spread the heart.
  • Bronwyn and her sister by bond, Gronwyn.
  • They have implements for cutting and they have some valuable potions.
  • Inside there's a fire and a pig being roasted.
  • The drow give the travellers some books to reutrn to the library beneath: a nonfiction book and a drow fairy tale.
  • They mention that the libararians are weird.
  • The party goes to the library. It is located at a waterfall. All the books are wet and damaged. The party desceneds dangerously to the library with the exception of Ahkesh who simply jumps down 3 stories unscathed.
  • Inside there is an upside down lighthouse. It looks Vermissian as it is made of rails from the railways.
  • The party descends a staircase
  • Asseyn touches some books and a huge metal creature shines a spotlight on him and starts moving toward him.
  • The party fights the automaton and slays it for loot. Much blood is spilled and many riches are retrieced.
  • The party goes deeper into the library
  • Aodh is informed that if he casts an effigy of himself into flame he can receive the red king's blessing.
  • The party enters a dry, nice part of the library with baroque architecture. Librarians greet them and say that they may only take out books if they bring stories. Ahkesh gives them his anthology of poems and Aodh gives them the scroll of the Corpse God.
  • Upon entering, Aodh starts spreading corpse god oil all over the library. He then sets fire to the library.
  • Ru'in knocks out Aodh and drags him out.
  • A drconit, maggotty, embryo suspended in the air is revealed as the illusion of the library is burned away. The haven is destroyed.
  • Aodh and Ru'in are mind-controlled into wanting to stay at the library.
  • Asseyn frees Aodh from the mind control
  • The party leaves and find Ru'in ambushed by 6 knolls wearing vermissian armor. They are talking into an orange orb.
