Meat in the Moon Passage

Date: May 13th, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • The surgeon of the God of Corpses, Galen-guides-the-pulse, can remove minor blood fallout.
  • Asseyn communed with the moon God. The God of Corpses rejected the magic.
  • Ahkesh and Ru'in catch the automaton clone as it falls. Ahkesh recites a nice poem and the Automaton attempts to learn poetry: direction, breath, mass, mirror.
  • Corpse God wants the story of its people brought to the library beneath.
  • Galen has religious valuable stuff, the corpse god has money in its lung. The corpse god wants to spread the story of its people.
  • Directly above Aodh is a butthole. In addition to butt fluids, a paper-like thing comes down. It is shitting our a parchment. It is the skin of something. It has writing of it. Aodh is instructed to deliver the story of his people to the Library Beneath.
  • The automaton names himself, "Vector" after receiving permission to do so from Ahkesh.
  • Asseyn needs to take something to The Temple Beneath on behalf of the tripartite Moon Goddess.
  • Aodh gives the God of Corpses a Grail charm made of wyvern-bone (The only keepsake I have from my biological parents. It was worn around my neck when I was an infant dropped off at an orphanage.)
  • People live inside the god of corpses. There's a market inside in the lung. Supply stress is relieved for the whole party as the townfolk are impressed with the new prophet of the God of Corpses, Aodh.
  • Asseyn interrogates the Corpse God Prophet. Ru'in and Ahshin learn of a tumor within the Corpse God's mouth that is a source of blood rot.
  • Hermit-looking woman, "Mave", I too am a fellow prophet. She says the Temple beneath is down. She says she can tell where the Library Beneath is in a days time.
  • Ahkesh debates the automaton and convinces them that the Four Body Problem IS possible. The automaton is overwhelmed, humbled, and reluctantly agrees.
  • Ru'in pipe bombs the tumor that is the source of blood rot. Asseyn assists. A passage beneath is revealed that smells like wet dust. The magic from there smells like wine (Moon Goddess).
  • Ru'in states that blood rot is flesh that has eyes coming out of it.
  • The party descends into the passageway and its covered by hands protruding from the walls that fondle and attack the party.
  • The party manages to find a secret passageway to escape the hands. They enter a room with a large, slender humanoid - about twice as tall as a normal human. It makes a hoot.
  • Aodh uses the Greed of the Red King to discern its deepest desires in this moment. It craves fresh meat.
  • The party gives it some fresh meat supplies from their supplies to distract it. Ru'in, Ahkesh, and Aodh grab items.
  • Ru'in attempts to read the beast's Drow writing on the wall with Ahkesh assisting.
  • Aodh stays behind to grab another item.
  • Ru'in sets a pipe bomb by the beast.
  • The beast detects the wall readers and starts attacking the party.
  • The party takes a lot of damage and the beast does not seem very injured by our attacks. The party beats a hasty retreat. Ru'in falls unconscious and is carried away.
