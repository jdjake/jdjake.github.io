Magi-Mal and Below

Date: June 17th, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • Her name is Ma-dgem-el? Mel, but people call her Magimel (because of the Magic)
  • Aodh decides to join her brood through a blood ritual
  • Aodh is now her adopted son. It is difficult for him to disobey her.
  • Asseyn investigates whether the people in Magimel's village are enslaved or mind-controlled. They are not. They revere her of their own free will. They believe that she can protect them from death and appreciate the power she gave them.
  • Asseyn seeks the pool that reflects the moon.
  • Aodh asks Magimel about the mirror that reflects the moon
  • The great Mashuk was destroyed many ages ago
  • Ahkesh learns about the Great Resonance Chamber, where maybe he could both hear the universe, and talk back to it.
  • Vector stays behind to gather information from the Dollmakers.
  • The party is wrapped up by the spiders and then through the inter-web arrives into a skycraper from the sky
  • There is a human guy with many knives stabbed in him, that grins and walks towards the party.
  • The party fights and murders him after taking some minor damage.
  • The party befriends a Xonic firebird that can guide them through this mysterious place
  • Asseyn superglues a rope across buildings to help the group move around
  • The group sees the book "Rituals of the Heart", which escaped from Ahkesh's things. Aodh used some spider silk to get to the building with the book
  • The team encounters a pair of ghosts that are not ready to move on. Asseyn convinces them to go to the afterlife. He is touched by them, and sees a horrifying glimpse of the afterlife.
  • The group continues into a very hot temple. Volcanic in nature. People walk around barefoot like in hot coals
