The Chair

Date: October 25th, 2026

Players: Avi, Gary


Characters

Avi played Avi.

Gary played Gary and Jacob.

Summary

  • At Home Depot, getting ready for a party. Needed a 500ft Aux Cable for a projector. Also bought a two pack of fluorescent tube lights. Gary bought a hot dog and a Red Bull from the register.
  • As we passed the precipice of the sliding doors to leave Home Depot, found ourselves in a dull and decayed room, the entrance to a house. It was completely dark, but we used our phone flashlights to illuminate around us. The automatic doors behind us were replaced by a single warped front door and two windows looking out onto a lawn we didn't recognize. In the middle of the room sat a chair, watching us. It was made of pulsing flesh, with eyes and mouths writhing across its surface, and it gurgled faintly like something choking on something. Gary flicked the light switch by the door, and a light flickered on and off in an upstairs window, visible from the lawn through the front window. It felt like the house was signaling someone out in the dark. There were doors to the West, North, and East, and a staircase going straight into a flat ceiling.
  • Gary fed the chair his Red Bull. It poured over the seat and pooled underneath. Gary then tried to carry the chair into the room to the north, through the double action doors, drawn by a smell of delicious food. But the chair returned to its place in the entrance room.
  • A ten-seat table stretched across the room under windows that showed corpses melting in the rain, with a faint rainbow hanging over them. The chair from the entrance was one of the ten seats at the table. Another seat was occupied by a benignly smiling man, sipping from a tea cup to fill his mouth, spitting what he drank back into the tea cup, and repeating. Macaroni and Cheese and Panang Curry sat at the table, some of Avi and Gary's favourite foods. But the two resisted eating the food. To the west there was a door with a grill window, and to the east, a door labelled "WC".
  • When we talked to the man, he repeated "I WORK HERE" louder, then louder again, standing and approaching. Ignoring him made him sit and sip tea until his mouth was full, then spit it back into the cup and start over, again and again. Eventually, we goaded him too many times, and he chased us out of the room, shouting "I WORK HERE" at an ear splitting volume, causing our eyes to bleed. When we returned, he sat at the dining room table again, drinking tea. We talked to him again, and when he stood up, we rearranged the chairs so he'd sit on the fleshy one. When he did, he vanished.
  • A tiny room with a sink and a tight shower stall. The toilet was like the chair in the other rooms, fleshy, with the toilet lid a fleshy maw. The shadow of a man cleaning himself was illuminated behind the shower curtain.
  • To the west of the Dining room, through a push door with a wired-glass window, we found a windowless filthy kitchen. Roach leavings speckled the counters and bugs skittered in and out of a sink full of rotting dishes. The oven (fleshy and gnawing like the chairs in the other room), when opened, was stuffed to the brim with sharp, charred bones. A pot of pink slime boiled on the stove. A vent in the northeast corner was the only opening in the room, the only escape for the oven smoke.
  • The door to the West of the entrance was metal, and its handle was incredibly hot to the touch. Inside, it felt like an oven. Outside, sick plants and garbage lay scattered around a freshly filled human-length hole with a rusty shovel sticking out of it. Stacks of yellowed newspapers and magazines filled the room, with a hammer resting on one pile and rusty golf clubs below the vent. The door locked behind us, and when we tried it, the doorknob came off in our hands. We used the chair to escape. Avi found himself smushed into the walls of the house, and had to break our way out.
  • Meanwhile, Gary had disappeared. Jacob, shopping at B&Q (for a 50-pack of metal lawn flamingos), found himself trapped in the house with Avi. Avi and Jacob found Gary in the dining room table, attempting to futilely drink soup with a fork. He responds only with "Sorry, we're closed" upon talking to him. Looks like he'd been lost to the house.
  • We broke down the door to the East of the entrance. Brown and deep red stains covered every surface, rotten clothing littered the floor, and an armoire had been hacked to bits. A figure lay curled in the fetal position on the bed, buried under layers of filthy blankets. The closet on the north wall had no doorknob, so we smashed it. Inside, among tattered coats, was an old circus mallet, humming faintly.
  • We went up the staircase and smashed open the attic, into a suffocatingly small, musty crawl space full of trash and bones. It was pitch black except for a faint glow on top of a trash pile: a device that looked like twelve different blades bolted to a motor.
  • We placed the blade device underneath the chair, and it snapped off one of the chair's legs. The chair lashed out and attacked Gary, who is already beyond saving. Avi set the blade motor device on the chair again, tearing it to shreds while it screamed in pain.
  • The windows all smashed at once, and the front door blew wide open onto the night. Avi returned to Home Depot. Jacob pushed Gary through the door, still holding his fork.
  • Avi has a huge boil growing over his eye. Jacob can no longer cut flesh without it cutting himself, so he has to be vegetarian from now on. But they're still sane, mostly. Gary returned to the normal world, but never mentally recovered.
