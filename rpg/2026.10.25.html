Stopover at the Shelterwood Inn

Date: October 26th, 2026

Players: Henry, Liam, Jameson


Characters

Henry played Henry.

Liam played Liam.

Jameson played Jameson.

Summary

  • On a road trip to Boston for a ballroom dance competition. Car breaks down on a lonely stretch of forest in Canada. There's something in the woods. A beast exuding shadowy mold, its shape barely distinguishable from the darkness around it. Jameson fought it by himself while the others fled, but quickly realized it'd be smarter to run for it. It roared as it followed them through the trees.
  • The trio reached a decaying roadside motel glowing beneath a neon sign - The Shelterwood Inn. After breaking down the front door in their panic, they were greeted by Otis, the bumbling owner, who insisted they calm down and leave everything until morning. Nearby, a priest scrubbed a dark red stain from the floor. A sheriff came in to inspect the fuss - said there'd been reports of bears raiding the dumpster behind the motel. Otis made Jameson a truly abysmal Manhattan (he seemed pretty incompetent). Nothing about the place felt right. A businesswoman was also staying at the inn, supposedly on her way to Coldwater.
  • Otis escorted the crew to the Honeymoon Suite, complete with a heart-shaped hot tub and sunken lounge (but only a bed for two?). Then he locked them inside, with a key that didn't fit the lock of the suite. Fortunately, Henry knew how to pick locks. After unlocking the suite door, Henry used the deadbolt to make sure nothing could get at us without breaking down the door.
  • Liam sent his drone out through the window, surveying the grounds. Down a weedy hill sat a convenience store and gas station, its lights dim - probably closed.
  • Jameson climbed out the window and descended toward the station to investigate. One of the gas hoses lay hanging, days old dried petrol underneath. Was this gas station even in use?
  • Meanwhile, someone knocked on the Honeymoon Suite door. A neighbor - he claimed to have had an accident and asked for help cleaning up. Strange - the owner said there wasn't any other guests at the motel for the night.
  • The convenience store's back door had been ripped from its hinges, leaving a jagged opening into the darkness. Liam guided the drone inside and discovered the shadow-mold beast (a bear?) feeding on the gas station attendant. The crew used the drone to lure the creature away from the building.
  • As Henry watched, a human figure approached the suite's open window, its body threaded with black veins and crawling shadow-mold. Henry reached the window just in time to stop it getting inside. The thing fled into the night. Jameson returned, finding a drainage pipe on the hill torn loose, as though something had forced its way through.
  • From the suite, the crew watched Otis, the priest, the sheriff, and the businesswoman gather weapons: knives, guns, shotguns, and a strange metal briefcase marked 'Containment'. They moved into the back rooms of the lobby. The crew waited until the parking lot appeared clear, then moved in. Screams erupted from somewhere inside.
  • Crew used the drone to inspect the parking lot. Screams from the lobby. Went to check them out once the coast was clear.
  • Henry climbed through the back of the lobby into what appeared to be the owner's residence. Most of it had been cleared out, but a half-eaten dinner sat on the table, abandoned for days beneath a cloud of flies. A tablet lay nearby, its lock screen displaying a smiling couple posing in front of the inn (not the 'owner' the trio had met).
  • Went into basement of back rooms of Motel. An injured figure lay crumpled in a corner beside a heavy grate, shoved aside to reveal the darkness below. Blood trickled into the grate. They approached to help. It was an ambush. A shadow-mold-infested human attacked them - beneath the mold, it looked like the man on the ipad lock screen. They fought it off and descended through the grate.
  • Beneath the inn lay an old brick cistern filled with waist-high, stagnant water. Gunshots echoed through the chamber. The four motel occupants had been cornered, and the sheriff floated unconscious in the water, his body veined with black mold. Above them, an enormous mass of living darkness clung to the ceiling. At its heart was the head of a teddy bear, swollen with mold. Its presence radiated terror.
  • Liam threw flashbangs into the chaos while Henry seized the sheriff's car keys. They managed to get the priest and businesswoman out, abandoning 'The Owner'. The crew fled toward the parking lot, hoping the sheriff's car would carry them to safety.
  • The beast emerged from the woods again. Henry rammed the car into it while the businesswoman bolted for her black SUV, only to discover it was covered in black mold, including the engine compartment. Now they didn't have a way out. Shit.
  • Other cars in parking lot also swarming with mold. Shit.
  • Found key on mangled body of gas station attendant, letting us in to the adjacent repair shop. Inside, they found another car, but its engine was coated in mold. Shit.
  • Ran back to car to push it out of the mud. The shadow-things pursued them through the darkness. Jameson stayed behind to hold them off. Henry drove toward the creatures, trying to create an opening for him to escape. But Henry also struck Jameson with the car. Shit.
  • Drove over Jameson again to make sure he was dead, then drove off as fast as they could. With the immediate danger behind them, the priest and the businesswoman revealed they were agents of the Bureau of Paranatural Investigations. The operation had gone to shit. Now that Henry and Liam knew too much, the agents made it clear they had no choice but to join The Bureau. Shit.
