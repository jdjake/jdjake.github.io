A Shattered Bowl

Date: June 24th, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • The group starts in this very hot temple. It seems to be an old temple for the Moon goddess below.
  • Adine is there, the mentor of Asseyn and the friend of Ahkesh. In drow communities, the parents rarely take care of the hatchlings, and Adine took care of raising Asseyn
  • Adine had a vision of Asseyn to build an artifact to inspire the worship of the drow. But the visions didn't stop. It shows you what you think you need, but it is a twisted version. He claims that the Moon beneath is not here, just a cursed version formed by what the people who seek it believe in.
  • Adine warns Ahkesh that the Heart is not the way to his ultimate goal. But Ahkesh refuses to listen, dismissing his old friend as just another person that turned against him, that didn't believe in him. Ahkesh tells him to spread his poems up above in the surface. Adine leaves with no other words.
  • There is a temple with big doors. Asseyn tries to open it but can't, as it is locked and the handles burn him. The woman there warns that the party is not worthy enough to enter the temple. They haven't suffered enough. She wants the party to crawl in their hands and knees, with no shoes, to show how they will suffer for the goddess.
  • Everybody is sick. The party accepts the challenge and defeats the old lady as Aodh licks a burning coal, and the lady can't surpass that. The team enters and finds that they can throw something into the pit to alleviate echo stress.
  • The party goes to Aodh's magic realm to get some stress off. Aodh takes a baby-sized statue of himself, after hearing a voice speak to him to take it.
  • Asseyn needs to "dig" to find the tears of the goddess. He gets a spoon, so actual digging, not just spiritual digging. Ahkesh builds a drill using the 13 cursed knives and the perpetual motion machine.
  • The party sees that the lava flows up, instead of down. They follow up to the volcano where Aodh hears from the Red King himself, who tells him to cast himself away, and let him in. Aodh throws his statue down the volcano and explodes in flames that go up in heat, going from orange, to red, to blue, to purple. He turns dragon-like, as the Red King is a powerful dragon. Aodh even gets wings.
  • The Red King speaks through Aodh's voice. He says: "I have seen you offer me so many gifts. The Marketplace... The Library... I granted you these gifts, and exchange you must use them to consume it ALL, I WANT IT ALL". Aodh tries to overgreed him, but he is overwhelmed by the all-powerful greed of the Red King. He becomes more Red King than Aodh in a way.
  • Asseyn sees through his soul vision how Aodh's souls seems to go out, and a big, majestic soul takes over. Is that Aodh's soul or the Red King's soul?
  • The party falls into the big hole now created by the drill. The gravity shifts inside, till it is a horizontal tunnel. Chimes sound in the distant. It looks like the surface of the moon.
  • Around there seem to be stone giants meditating. With all stone great swords.
  • The party needs to pass 6 trials, one for each attribute of the tripartite goddess: Community, Sagacity, Fury, Grace, Tenacity and Vigilance.
  • Asseyn starts, trying to convince the giants about our sense of Community and our adventures together while quoting scripture. The giant stands and starts to walk.
  • Aodh attacks the Fury giant with increasing flame. It succeeds
  • Ru'in tries to meditate after the show of Fury. He partially succeeds
  • Ahkesh finds a giant that seems to have gone missing. He finds the giant of Vigilance and gets a moon rock.
  • Asseyn shows bravery almost sacrificing his hand, but he succeeds against the giant of Tenacity.
  • Ahkesh recites a poem about the sky to the pensive giant of Grace, who looks at the black sky clearly wishing for a bright blue sky. He is able to transfer the knowledge gracefully.
  • The giants lead us to a place where Ru'in stabs the weird rock, making it bleed. He captures the liquid hoping it is the tears of the goddess. It makes the light do all kind of stuff, mainly refractive, not reflective.
  • The surface is like egg yolk and it quivers with pain after the stabbing.
  • The whole surface is a very big soul. Asseyn tries to get more "blood" from the creature but the yolk cannot take it and it quivers.
  • The moon creature wants to care for its followers and it is waiting to hatch so that it can take care of its followers even more.
  • Asseyn decides that "we have come far enough, it must be for a reason". But he can't, and he yells out to the heavens "I CAN'T!!!", and the bowl falls to the side, shattering. And Asseyn walks back to the temple.
  • Asseyn decides to destroy the pit from where the clay of the mirror bowl is made. He drops his lantern in the middle, making the fire spread and melting all the clay into glass, stopping the pit forever.
