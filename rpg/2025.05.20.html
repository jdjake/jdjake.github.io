The Festival of Sannon

Date: May 20th, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • We enter out of the catacombs and find ourself in a fairly wide open space. In front of us is a huge cathedral-looking building. It is made mostly of stone but is filled in with a patchwork of whatever random materials the villages had on hand.
  • The church is busy. It is filled with Pilgrims as identified by Asseyn. They are followers of the Moon Beneath. They wear simple clothing and their only adornments are necklaces of the tripartite moon goddess. They are not wearing shoes. Their feet are worn by travel and humility - a sign of their piety.
  • Waterfalls of fluid gush from nearby. This is interpreted as a good omen.
  • Time is indicated in the Heart by the pulses of the Heart.
  • Asseyn seeks a particular artifact: Maeshuk. It is a reflective mirror. It is the artifact of reflections mentioned by the genie, Maruk.
  • The cathedral is made of stone. It also contains mithril.
  • A priestess of the tripartite moon goddess, Sister Griswald, is casting water droplets on pilgrims.
  • She is motherly and caring. She gives vibes of "leave your woes and cares at the door".
  • Ahkesh chooses to participate in the festival.
  • Sister Griswald offers to take care of Ru'in
  • The party gets the opportunity to heal fortune, echo, or blood stress at various shrines.
  • The pilgrims lays eggs in dried entrails. In the world above they use dried hay.
  • Ahkesh and Victor participate in the sacrifice of blood to the Tripartite Moon Goddes but Ahkesh does not truly believe in her. He believes in the power of the djins.
  • Ahshin makes a small stab into his thumb using a ceremonial blade and lets it flow into the pool already there.
  • Ahshin tries to convince everyone that Victor is not a heretic despite not being able to bleed. Everyone is shocked and doesn't believe Ahshin. He loses standing in the community. The party is asked to leave before things get ugly.
  • Ru'in awakens.
  • Aodh casts "Greed of the Red King" on Sister Griswald. The party learns that what sister Griswald wants most of all is the health and wellbeing of her people. We learn that she specifically want medication that her people desperately need.
  • The druid gangs are stopping medicine shipments.
  • The infected are gathering in the Green Station, an abandoned Amusement Park
  • The party heads to the meditative pond.
  • Ahshin lights up a silver brazier in celebration of the group's adventures in the cathedral and sways it back and forth. The party let's the moon goddess' vapors enter their lungs.
  • The party gets inverted into themselves and appears in a cave with many tunnels branching off.
  • Everything seems inverted.
  • Ahshin uses soul vision. Three humanoid figures are detected.
  • No magical smells are detected. Only normal, metallic scents.
  • The party decides to head toward the three souls. Ahshin leads the party.
  • 2 humans and a gnoll are foud and they are afraid. They hide from the party beneath cardboard boxes.
  • Aodh scares off one of them after lifting their box.
  • The remaining two stay behind and are observed to be covered in horns and their bodies seem grotesquely misfigured.
  • Ahshin attempts to discern their issues. They are highly psychologically distressed. The heart seems to be the source of their distress.
  • They have an overwhelming desire to build mazes and traps.
  • Ru'in and Ahshin manage to calm one down - at a cost.
  • The party finds out the safe way throough the labyrinth.
  • The party encounters a placehold that requires a flame. Aodh uses a lighter that burns his soul to light it. It projects beams.
  • Ahshin leads the party in directing the light. The party succeeds in illuminating a path.
  • The party does it again successfully and a beam starts emitting a harmonic sound. The light focuses and shoots through rocks blocking a pathway. A new pathway is revealed.
  • The party decides to do it again. This time they are separated by illusion and it's risky. The party succeeds but at a cost. They successfully are un-inverted and escape the delve.
  • The party arrives at the town of "Labyrinth". They are in tier 2 of the Heart.
