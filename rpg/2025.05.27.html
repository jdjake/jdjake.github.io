Labyrinth

Date: May 27th, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • The party approaches a dense town. There's a large market. There are bits and bobs covering the town: knick knacks and things.
  • The party enters the market and searches for cool items.
  • Aodh buys a pair of fireproof cletes and a can of Corpse God (Whale) Oil. The Corpse God Oil is an undying, jade-green, necrotic flame that cannot be put out with water.
  • Ru'in searches for legendary items and finds a tent in the market with a d10 Cursed item: a mask of an aelfir that's been shot through with an arrow.
  • Strange people on the road to Red Cap Grove are spreading seeds of blood rot.
  • Ru'in attempts to steal the legendary cursed item and fails with minor fallout.
  • Ru'in evades an angry mob successfully. However, a group of ten continues to pursue him.
  • Ru'in attempts to scare them off with a pipe bomb unsuccessfully. They are injured and flee, but Ru'in is also injured.
  • The fire from the merchant's tent is spreading. throughout the market. The city is above and protected by walls. The fire is spreading up ropes that haphazardly lead up the walls from the market. The party cuts the ropes successfully to prevent the fire from spreading to the city.

  • Aodh goes to the Tavern: The Penrose Stairs (infinite Stairs).
  • The front entrance of the tavern is booby-trapped. Aodh is struck by the sign
  • Ahkesh ventures down the tunnel and is ambushed by a humongous creature as he peaks around the corner.
  • Aodh gets drunk and pees down the well
  • The party gathers and sleeps a night at the tavern
