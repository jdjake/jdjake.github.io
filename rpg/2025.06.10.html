Red Market

Date: June 10th, 2025

Players: Avi, Diego, Eric, Liam


Characters

Avi played Ru'in

Diego played Ahkesh

Eric played Asseyn

Liam played Aodh .

Summary

  • Ru'in has been captured by two bounty hunters! The two sisters from different mothers. They take press Ru'in to the ground with their boots.
  • Vector gets a minor fallout
  • Asseyn makes Gronwyn's shadow to stab itself (the shadow), that opens a wound in the woman's gut.
  • Ru'in stabs his fingers into the open wound, damaging Gronwyn. After this event, the other sisters' skin leaves her body revealing a monstrous form.
  • Ahkesh attacks the big figure, a dangerous action, but with the help of Asseyn manages to harm it.
  • The creature continues forward trying to attack Ru'in, but then Ru'in redirects its attention back to Ahkesh. Then, Ru'in throws a bag of circuits to Ahkesh, who uses this to create an occult energy taser to kill the monster
  • The two women's bodies are left in the floor, both dead. The party gains a number of objects, including a mythical Xinic bird's feathers shawl, a cursed knife, and a book about bloodrot.
  • Vector throws up a white liquid, and to help him, the group seeks out an occult sect dedicated to non-flesh humanoid creations like Vector, The Dollmakers.
  • The team enters an opening created by the sisters to take Ru'in for the reward. It is a big market full of people and dangerous capitalism.
  • A small, rusty-skinned child tries to pickpocket Ahkesh, but Ru'in with his mercenary intuition, catches the child an threatens her. She ends up helping the team moving through the crowds.
  • Vector gets in trouble with a guy playing cups and balls, but Ahkesh finds the trick with his phantom lenses.
  • Asseyn sees through the character of the people and helps the team navigate without being robbed.
  • The team finds a stall selling wooden dolls, like the ones the Dollmakers use. The team then realizes that a Dollmaker drow has been made into a slave! But Ru'in makes a deal to get the drow free for 12 hours in exchange of the lubricant liquid that came from Vector.
  • Estat, the Dollmaker slave, decides to help us, but we need to find a replacement lubricant. Estat has a wooden hand.
  • The party decides to buy the lubricant. They succeeded at a cost, and then they negotiated with the merchant to get the lubricant.
  • With it, Estat managed to repair Vector, and give the group a better made wooden doll to later show the Dollmaker group.
  • Estat was caught after making a deal to cross the market. He said he paid all the coins in his tunic, but the guy slipped an extra coin into his tunic before the day was over, making Estat break his contract and fall into slavery
  • The team frees Estat by convincing the owner that his name is actually Rani, which means "Free" in drow-speech. The owner realizes the trick after it happens though, and tells others in the market about it.
  • Aodh gets the people in the market together and makes a swift escape through his temple dimension
  • Asseyn makes a deal over a game of toss to get a secret about the Moon Beneath. He wins
  • A heart monkey, all blood-red, steals Estat's wooden hand. Ahkesh tracks him down and Estat gets his hand back.
  • The party reached the end of the market, finding train tracks, and then the distance to the market keeps expanding an expanding. Carnivorous-looking flowers pop-up. They are mammal-size, capable of chewing a whole animal. It is made up from the parts of other animals.
  • Aodh shows Vector how he can turn a stone into pure fire, which heavily intrigues Vector, who now wants to learn to turn things into fire like Aodh. They have a heart-to-heart conversation about being part of a community.
  • The new haven is part bloodrot, part the Moon beneath temple, covered in webs.
  • A drow descends from a spider silk strand. She has the body of a spider. She grins at the party mischiefously.
