Time

  • ROUNDs are used for COMBAT. 5-10 Seconds.
  • STRETCHs are used for STEALTH. 5-10 Minutes.
  • SHIFTS are used for RECOVERY. 5-10 hours.

Time is not specified exactly. In cases where strict time limits are required, use SUPPLY ROLLS to determine the passage of time.

Zones

A location is divided into ZONEs (each ZONE is typically a room or corridor in size). A ZONE is either CLUTTERED or OPEN. Borders between ZONES can be OPEN or BLOCKED (by wall or bulkhead). Everyone in a ZONE has line of sight of one another, unless this does not make narrative sense. OPEN ZONE borders don’t block line of sight.

Range is ADJACENT (next to you), SHORT (in same ZONE), MEDIUM (in adjacent ZONE), LONG (up to 4 ZONES away), and EXTREME (Farther).

Statistics

Attributes

Go from 1-5.

  • STRENGTH (STR): Raw Power.
  • AGILITY (AGI): Speed, Motor Skills.
  • WITS (WIT): Intelligence, Alertness.
  • EMPATHY (EMP): Charisma and Ability to Read Others.

Skills

Go from 0-5.

  • STR Skills: HEAVY MACHINERY, STAMINA, CLOSE COMBAT
  • AGI Skills: MOBILITY, RANGED COMBAT, PILOTING
  • WIT Skills: OBSERVATION, COMTECH, SURVIVAL
  • EMP Skills: COMMAND, MANIPULATION, MEDICAL AID

Stress

Represents Mounting Pressure. Mainly increases when rolls are PUSHed, but also:

  • When nearby PCs suffer certain STRESS RESPONSES
  • When a nearby NPC panics
  • When you fire a 2nd or 3rd burst of auto fire
  • When you become FATIGUED
  • When a member of your crew attacks you.
  • When a nearby person is revealed to be an android.
  • Any significantly horrible thing.

For each full STRETCH spent resting in a Safe area, decrease STRESS by 1, as well as removing a STRESS RESPONSE.

Resolve

Used to resist stress and panic. Starts at (WIT + EMP)/2, rounded up.

Health

Reduced by taking damage. Starts at (STR + AGI)/2, rounded up.

Encumbrance

Can carry 2×STR slots without problems. A full water bottle takes up about 1 slot. Some items may take half slots, double slots, or no slots at all (TINY items), depending on their size and weight. You can carry up to 4×STR items, but your STRESS increases by 1 each time you move during a STRETCH.

Fatigue

When you don’t get enough air, water, food, or sleep, or are exposed to extreme cold or heat, make a SURVIVAL roll at regular intervals. When failed, you become FATIGUED:

  • You can no longer recover HEALTH.
  • You must continue to make SURIVAL ROLLs at regular intervals. Each failure gives +1 DAMAGE and +1 STRESS.
  • If BROKEN from FATIGUE, don’t roll for a CRITICAL INJURY. Instead, make DEATH ROLLs at the same interval as the previous SURVIVAL ROLLs. Only removing the source of FATIGUE will stop these rolls.

Examples:

  • If your supply of air runs out, make a roll every ROUND.
  • After 24 hours without food or water, make a SURVIVAL roll every SHIFT.
  • After 24 hours without sleep, make a SURVIVAL roll per shift. If BROKEN, you don’t die, but pass out and sleep for a shift (impossible to wake).
  • Hypersleep makes you fatigued, remedied after one SHIFT, assuming you eat a sturdy meal.

Resolution Systems

Basic Resolution System

If a character does something risky, when under pressure, or when something is truly at stake, test a relevant SKILL ROLL. Roll a # of BASE DIE equal to SKILL + ATTRIBUTE + MODIFIER + HELP, as well as a number of STRESS DIE, equal to your current stress level.

  • If you get any 6s, you succeed. If not, you fail.
  • For each addition 6 rolled, gain a bonus effect. Perhaps you do something quicker, quieter, or with an unexpected effect. Negotiate with the GM for these effects.
  • If you roll a 1 on a STRESS DICE, and the SKILL ROLL is not PASSIVE, you suffer a STRESS RESPONSE. Roll a die, modified by STRESS - RESOLVE, and compare to the STRESS RESPONSE table. If you get a stress response you already have, gain +1 STRESS instead.
  • Up to 3 PCs and NPCs may HELP you, if it makes narrative sense. It takes their action to do so, and each person helping adds a DIE to your roll.
  • You may PUSH your roll. Take +1 STRESS and reroll all non 6s. You can only PUSH once per check.
  • You may spend STORY POINTs to get +1 6 to a roll, which you can add after rolling. You can never have more than 3 STORY POINTs at a time.
   
Stress Response
0 You Keep Cool. No Effect.
1 Jumpy. When you push skill roll, gain +2 STRESS rather than +1.
2 Tunnel Vision. All WIT rolls get -2 die.
3 Aggrevated. All EMP rolls get -2 die.
4 Shakes. All AGI rolls get -2 die.
5 Frantic. All STR rolls get -2 die.
6 Deflated. Cannot push any skill rolls.
7+ Mess Up. Your action fails regardless of # Sucesses, and gain +1 STRESS

Negotiation Skill Rolls

Each of the following factors gives you +1 die:

  • More people on your side.
  • What you ask for doesn’t cost the other anything.
  • Opponent is damaged.
  • You’ve helped opponent previously.
  • You present your case well.

Each of the following factors gives -1 die:

  • Less people on your side.
  • What you ask for is valuable or dangerous.
  • Opponent has nothing to gain by helping you.
  • You are having trouble understanding each other.
  • You are talking via radio or shouting at a distance.

Opposed Rolls

  • Both sides make a relevant SKILL ROLL. Whoever gets more 6s wins, with the ‘active’ side winning on a tie. In the case of PVP, active PC decides to push roll first.
    • If more than one party on each side, each character makes a roll, and the highest counts for their side.

Supply Roll

Supply Roll: Roll a die equal to your current rating (max. 6 dice). For each 1 you roll, supply rating decreases by one.

  • AIR: After a STRETCH, or strenuous activity.
  • AMMO: After firing.
  • POWER: After use (or description).
  • TIME: After each unit of time.

GM doesn’t roll for NPC supplies, just decrease supply by one when necessary.

Panic Roll

Make a Panic Roll when:

  • a nearby PC becomes BROKEN.
  • You see a Xenomorph for the first time.
  • A Xenomorph comes within ADJACENT range.
  • You witness another PC suffer certain PANIC RESPONSEs.
  • A truly horrifying event occurs.

Roll 1d6 + STRESS - RESOLVE, and consult the following table:

   
Stress Response
0 Keep Cool. No effect.
1 Spooked. +1 Stress
2 Noisy. Any characters nearby are instantly alerted to your presence.
3 Twitchy. Make an immediate SUPPLY ROLL for either AIR, AMMO, or POWER (GM Choice).
4 Lose a weapon or important item (GM Choice). In combat, pick up as a QUICK ACTION. Out of combat, requires an OBSERVATION ROLL and a STRETCH to find.
5 Paranoid. Cannot give or recieve help until effect ends.
6 Hesitant. Get #10 Initiative card each COMBAT ROUND.
7 Frozen by fear. Lose next turn, and cannot perform any INTERRUPT ACTIONs until next turn.
8 Seek cover. Immediately seek FULL cover in CLUTTERED ZONE, as an INTERRUPT ACTION. Once in cover, lose 1 STRESS , and cannot perform any INTERRUPT ACTIONs until next turn.
9 Scream. Scream your lungs out. Lose next turn + no INTERRUPT ACTIONs. Lose 1 STRESS, but every friendly PC in ZONE must make a PANIC ROLL.
10 Flee. Immediately move away from source of panic, into adjacent ZONE, if such a MOVE is possible, as an INTERRUPT ACTION. Lose 1 STRESS afterwards, but all friendly PCs in starting ZONE gain 1 STRESS. If you cannot move, become CATATONIC (result 12) instead.
11 Frenzy. Immediately attack nearest person or creature. Every friendly PC in ZONE must make a PANIC ROLL. You won’t stop until target is BROKEN, or until PANIC stops. You cannot perform INTERRUPT actions before then.
12 Catatonic. You’re doomed! You collapse to the floor, and can’t move, rambling or staring blankly into oblivion, until your PANIC stops.

Some PANIC EFFECTS are immediate. Otherwise, they remain until you are BROKEN, for one STRETCH, or until another character succeeds at a COMMAND ROLL (a full action in combat).

If you roll a 9+ on a PANIC ROLL, immediately make an EMPATHY roll. If failed, roll a d66 and gain a MENTAL TRAUMA.

Procedures

Stealth Procedure

In a single STRETCH, PCs take an action, then NPCs take an action. They may MOVE to an adjacent ZONE, SEARCH their current ZONE, KEEP GUARD, or HIDE in a CLUTTERED ZONE. A Motion tracker may be used in addition to a normal action, but requires a POWER SUPPLY ROLL.

Moving

If characters MOVE into the line of sight of another character, make a PASSIVE OPPOSED OBSERVATION ROLL (-2 die if in DARK ZONES, +2 die if KEEP GUARD).

If OBSERVATION ROLL is tied, start the Combat Procedure. If one side wins the OBSERVATION ROLL, they may choose to:

  • Reveal Themselves:
  • Ambush the Enemy. Begin Combat Rounds with #1 Initiative, and +2 Die to initial Attack (cannot be dodged or defended against).
  • HIDE in the current ZONE.
  • If MOVING, BACK OUT the way they came.

Searching

Thoroughly searching a ZONE may take one or more STRETCHES. Make an OBSERVATION SKILL ROLL. Each 6 reveals one hidden character or item in a ZONE.

SEARCHing a ZONE may recover hidden characters. If so, start the Combat Procedure.

Hiding

Take a STRETCH to carefully hide in a CLUTTERED ZONE, or to HIDE an item. HIDDEN Characters are not detected when in line of sight of NPCs. On a later STRETCH, you may choose to unhide, and take any usual action. When doing so, before taking an action, if any characters are in line of sight, a PASSIVE OPPOSED OBSERVATION ROLL is made as with MOVING into line of sight.

Combat Procedure

Each round, draw initiative to determine order. On your turn, you can HOLD OFF to force another to act in your place. Otherwise, you perform a FULL ACTION and a QUICK ACTION, or two QUICK ACTIONs. A QUICK ACTION includes movement, and can be used out of turn order as an INTERRUPT ACTION.

Full Action Examples:

  • Attack in Close Combat
  • Make a Ranged Attack
  • Jump or Climb
  • Take Full Cover
  • Interact with a Terminal
  • Use Machinery
  • Give First Aid
  • Clear a Jammed Weapon
  • Persuade Someone
  • Bark Orders
  • Drive a Vehicle

Quick Action Examples:

  • Move into an adjacent ZONE.
  • Move from SHORT to ADJACENT.
  • Move to a door or hatch and peek through.
  • Lock a door or hatch.
  • Take partial cover.
  • (!) Defend against a close combat attack.
  • Aim a ranged weapon.
  • (!) Dodge a ranged weapon.
  • Reload a Weapon.
  • Pick up an item from the ground.
  • Rally a Broken Character.

Bark Orders

ROLL COMMAND. For each success, the target gets +1 die to a ROLL relevant to the command given.

Break Down Doors

Close Combat Attack with +2 Dice (Typical Metal Door AP 2 and can take 10 points of damage). Electronic locks can be opened with COMTECH ROLL.

Blocking

Can choose to block another character moving past you. Must make a special CLOSE COMBAT action to get paast you.

Dragging a Person

Requires STAMINA ROLL to move between ZONEs.

Leaving Combat

If you move out of sight of your opponents, and it’s not sure where you went, must pass OBSERVATION ROLL to continue combat.

Close Combat Roll

Roll CLOSE COMBAT ROLL. If succeed, inflict WEAPON DAMAGE + #6s. Can aim at weak spot, choosing to make CLOSE COMBAT ROLL with -2 Dice to reduce AP by 1.

Defending

In Close combat, can defend as a quick action by saying so when attack is declared. Make opposed CLOSE COMBAT ROLL.

Special Attacks

Make a normal CLOSE COMBAT ROLL, which can be defended against. Example Special Attacks:

  • Disarm: Wrestle an item away, keeping it or throwing it into ADJACENT ZONE.
  • Pass: Move past enemy blocking movement.
  • Shove: Push from ADJACENT to SHORT, or to shove through hatch.
  • Grapple: Pin Enemy. They must take a full action to attempt to break away from you via an opposed CLOSE COMBAT ROLL. While grappling, can make an unarmed attack that cannot be defended against.

Restrained or Unaware Targets

Get +2 die to roll.

Firearms in Close Combat

Use CLOSE COMBAT instead of RANGED COMBAT, typically with -2 Dice. Target can defend.

Ranged Attack Modifiers

Can spend a quick action and full action to AIM, getting +2 die on ranged attack.

Other Modifiers:

  • Partial Cover: -2 Die
  • Full Cover: -3 Die
  • Large Target: +2 Die
  • Small Target: -2 Die

Dodging

Roll MOBILITY to dodge RANGE ATTACK.

Friendly Fire

If miss attack against someone ADJACENT to another, roll 2 die, inflicting damage for each 6.

Conserving Ammo

Can spend a 6 on RANGED ATTACK to conserve ammo. Cannot conserve single shot weapons.

Full Auto

Full auto weapons can be fired successively without taking another action up to three times, if each before is a success and non-pushed. Each attack beyond the first gives +1 STRESS. You cannot consume ammo. Aiming carefully only gives advantage to first attack roll.

Armor Piercing

Armor counts as one less than usual against such attacks.

Stun Damage

Does not do damage. Instead, after getting attacked, pass STAMINA ROLL (- # of 6s) or lose your next turn. This continues each turn until you pass a STAMINA ROLL.

Broken

If you reach 0HP you are BROKEN. Roll on CRITICAL DAMAGE TABLE. If you aren’t dead, someone can administer FIRST AID (full action), or RALLY you with a COMMAND ROLL (quick action) to give you +1HP per success, or you RECOVER to 1HP after a SHIFT. Any further damage taken while BROKEN gives another CRITICAL INJURY.

If you have a LETHAL injury, after the given time, you must make succeed STAMINA ROLL (not pushable + no STRESS DICE). You must do this again after another period of time has elapsed. If you SUCEED three times, you stabilize. Alternatively, FIRST AID can be administered as a Full Action (on yourself with -2 Die).

The effect last until Healing Time has passed. Sometimes Surgery is needed as well.
