Session 19

31st October, 2026

Morning of the Day of the Falcon (Day 27)

Players:

  • Hayley (Playing Evander)
  • Alec (Playing Nied)
  • Henry (Playing Mavis)
  • Thomas (Playing Jef)

Summary:

Went through one full take of the Bathhouse Illusion:

  • Copper League - all wear copper feathered headdresses. Asking about The General in the theater. People are placing wads of wax in their ears in the back. Raywish cloaks concentrated in the front.
  • Meltalia greeted by Melody-Begets-Dreaming, who gifts her a diamond tiara on behalf of the Starfall.
  • The Island used to be called the Isle of Light by the Elves.
  • ‘The Archon of Appetites’, a member of the ‘Cult of the Veil’ introduced herself to Meltalia.
  • Lord Seven-Steps watched from afar uncomfortably. He shivered and waled with purpose to greet Meltalia. Possessed? Whispered “Consecrate”, which activated a gem on the tiara. Lord Seven-Steps-Before-Dawn .
  • Stagehands given spice in syringes from a high-ranking elf (Called “Blood-Upon-Wedding-Lace). Given to them ‘on behalf of the Starfall’. She then magics snoring sounds over their dead bodies, after moving them to a break room. Says “Hollow the Vessel” over the corpses.
  • Blood-Upon-Wedding-Lace got backstage with a porcelain chime struck with a metal rod that unlocked backstage.
  • Thunder-Kisses-Stone telepathically sends a message to Blood-Upon-Wedding-Lace. He got stuck in cold storage and needs to get out.
  • Cold storage is like a morgue, Thunder-Kisses-Stone was in a drawer blocked by an urn full of orange needles.
  • Thunder is in a drawer blocked by a urn full of spice needles. He says “how much time do we have left to complete the transfer” Evidently not much because Blood rushes out of the room, and Thunder follows.
  • Old man dead in fountain in Tepidarium.
  • Blood rushes back into green room, talks to Meltalia. Blood-Upon-Wedding-Lace whispers “Renounce” to the tiara when in the dressing room, turning the fourth gem Sour Emerald.
    • 2 other gems we missed, one gem left to color at this point
  • Stagehand whispers last word “Scour” to color fifth gem Black Obsidian.
    • Stagehand has slight limp and smells of an ointment
  • Showtime!
    • 2 Statues of Meltalia wearing the tiaras, one seated on a thing, another holding an amphora
    • Gift from one of the patrons
    • Someone limp runs into the middle isle at the last second, people happily let them in.
    • Meltalia sings while pouring Nerieds out of her jug. Her singing is ethereal, inhuman. It sounds like a glass harmonica, like the resonance of crystal cups.
    • Crowd sings along, and Meltalia is pleased. On the second song the crowd sings a strange corruption of the song (a strange forward-reversed singing, like the way the spirits talk in The Red Room).
    • Nereids get sucked from the stage into the paintings held up by audience members.
    • Meltalia hurls scalding water at the crowd, burning the front row alive, and screams her crystal song, causing organ damage to others.
    • Eventually we felt her soul rupture in two, and the time loop resets.

Went through a second take of the Bathhouse Illusion:

  • Step 1
    • Melody-Begets-Dreaming moves around seat reservations in the theater, shifting people to the front / back.
    • Robot statues place paintings under chairs
    • In cistern basement room we find an altar to a giant horse radiating swirling black energy that reminds us of our tattoos. Various catwalks and levers. Nothing happening right now.
    • Also storage rooms in the basement.
  • Step 2
    • Thunder-Kisses-Stone gets in cold storage, hides and animates carvings on the wall
    • Multiple representations of Thunder-Kissed-Stone crawl out, crawling along the walls. One goes through the shower elevator to the tepidarium. Wall spirit enters the spectacled guy and controls him. He then drown himself in the fountain
    • Sculptor for the statues “Came from Panoplia” and is said to be so skilled he must have petrified the statues from living beings. Melody lets Meltalia in on a secret. He brought you a gift! (though it’s meant to be a surprise)
    • Several fountains in the statue gardens are statues of us. Releasing water from all sorts of orifices… another exit from the simulation
    • Ant statues in dream as well.
    • While in the fountains, Meltalia is introduced to Dusty-Lovers-Tomb, who says “Sunder” to Meltalia and a diamond turns blood orange.
  • Step 3
    • A thin, impeccably dressed man arrives to greet Meltalia, named Velvet-Tongue-Lapping. Says “Ember” and a diamond turns Veinous Red.
    • Gives Meltalia a chime that helps her get backstage.
    • Body gets cleaned up upstairs
  • Step 4
    • Body gets dumped in the storage room
    • Archon of Appetites starts ritual in the ritual cistern in the basement
    • “The Copper League call us an abomination. They lack vision”.
    • The cult brings forth bodies to the ritual. They pray to ‘Our Lady’.
    • Brings forth new initiates - ‘Souls for the Womb of Serpents’.
    • Melody meets members of the Copper League and as a gesture of good faith on behalf of the Starfall gives them the best seats in the house. She says she wants to maintain the old Elvish custom that no violence can be conducted in a house of bathing. The Bathhouse will be a neutral ground in Hot Springs City.

Tips for Future sessions

  • Meltalia was given a magic tiara.
  • Tiara has 5 magic words said in this order to activate: “Consecrate”, “Sunder”, “Ember”, “Renounce”, “Scour”.
  • Meltalia was split in two and bound in two statues in concert hall of Bathhouse.
  • Maybe saying the words backwards to the statues will reverse the effect?
  • There are many Elf factions:
    • Copper League: Wears copper feathered headdresses. Refuses to wear Reywish. Refuse to do a lot of things.
    • Cult of the Veil: Worships statue in bathhouse basement, sacrifices people to it. Has relation to magic similar to our Curse.
    • The Starfall: Produce the spice drug?

Potential Leads for Future Sessions

  • Ritual room in the basement bodes checking out - has a connection to our curse
  • Meltalia’s soul is likely split in two and stuffed in two statues of her
  • Cold storage has spice in syringes, collect some drugs!
